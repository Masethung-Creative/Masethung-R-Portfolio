## The brief

NASSCOM and FutureSkills are national-level industry bodies, so the work carries
more scrutiny than internal collateral. Every asset had to hold up next to
their existing brand while still reading as an SFJ campaign.

## What I did

**Worked inside two brand systems at once.** Client guidelines set the outer
limits; SFJ's identity had to stay legible within them. Most of the craft here
is knowing which rules are load-bearing and which have give.

**Built for repeatability.** Campaign assets arrive in batches on short notice,
so I set up templates that hold the brand and let the content change — the only
way 4+ client assets and 40+ recruitment posters a month is survivable solo.
