## The work

Six months at Nexus Studio, NoBroker's in-house design team, running two
parallel streams: product catalogs and internal event campaigns.

**Catalog mockups — 70+ a month.** Interior design products rendered into
contexts customers could read at a glance. High volume, so the value was in
setting up a repeatable approach to layout and lighting rather than treating
each one as a fresh problem.

**Festival and event creatives — 15+ a month.** Office events and seasonal
campaigns, working directly with HR and Marketing.

## Working across four teams

Interior Design, Principal Design, HR, and Marketing all requested work with
different vocabularies and different definitions of done. The useful habit was
pinning down what "finished" meant at the brief stage instead of discovering it
during revisions.

## Brand consistency

Every piece went out under NoBroker's guidelines. At 500+ assets that isn't a
checklist step — it's the reason templates and shared components exist.
